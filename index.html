🎯 Project Overview

AI-generated nursery rhyme cartoons for YouTube, targeting toddlers and preschoolers (ages 1–5). Short, colourful, musical videos (~2 minutes each) featuring recurring animated characters — entirely produced using AI tools at a fraction of traditional animation costs.

The Opportunity: Kids' content is the most reliable and evergreen category on YouTube. Channels like CoComelon have billions of views. With AI tools, we can produce similar-quality content for ~£32/month — no animation studio, no voice actors, no expensive software.

Style References

  • CoComelon — 3D animated, bright colours, simple educational songs
  • Super JoJo — Cute baby/toddler characters, warm soft lighting
  • BabyBus — Vibrant colours, fun characters, repetitive catchy songs

Core Format

  • ~2 minute videos
  • Original songs with AI-generated vocals
  • 3D Pixar-style animated clips
  • Educational content (ABCs, numbers, colours, animals, weather)
  • Recurring characters for brand recognition

💡 Signature Concepts

🖥️ Tummy TV

Inspired by Teletubbies — Buddy has a screen on his tummy surrounded by a colourful flower border. The camera zooms into it and the nursery rhyme video plays "inside" the portal.

  • Instantly recognisable brand format
  • Natural transition device between segments
  • Nostalgic hook parents will recognise
  • Flexible — any content can play "inside" the portal

🚪 Door Reveal Format

A wall with colourful numbered doors. The girl character walks up, knocks, and the door opens to reveal the subject (fruit, animal, etc.). Camera zooms into the open door for a seamless transition.

  • Works for any topic — fruits, animals, vehicles, shapes, colours
  • Built-in suspense and excitement ("What's behind the door?")
  • Highly reusable — same door assets, different reveals
  • Each door is colour-coded (red, yellow, orange, purple)

🪟 Window Weather Format

The character looks out of a window (living room or classroom). The weather changes outside — sunny, rainy, snowy, windy. The character reacts to each one.

  • Teaches weather vocabulary and spelling
  • Beautiful visual variety (golden sunshine → cosy rain → magical snow → dynamic wind)
  • Colour splash transitions between weather types using paintbrush swipe

Character Prompt Tips (Lessons Learned)

🦷 Teeth: Use "closed-mouth smile" to avoid AI generating crazy teeth. If teeth needed, specify "only small simple top two front teeth"
🧸 Cuteness: Add "kawaii style, plush toy aesthetic, tiny dot eyes, rosy pink cheeks" for maximum cute, minimum scary
🔄 Consistency: Generate a favourite character image first, then use it as reference for all subsequent video clips
🍎 Faces on objects: If AI struggles with faces on fruit/objects, go faceless and add googly eyes in CapCut
🎨 Style anchors: Always include "3D Pixar-style" and "Pixar quality rendering" for consistent output
